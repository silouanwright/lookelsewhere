# Research Handoff: OmaRest Competitive Audit

## Goal

Produce a source-backed product and implementation comparison between OmaRest
and LookElsewhere.

## Current conclusion

OmaRest is an adjacent app-boundary tool rather than a direct substitute.
It leads on configurable per-app continuous-use rules and deliberate override
friction. LookElsewhere leads on eye-strain scheduling breadth, context
awareness, planned/natural breaks, Omarchy integration, keyboard interaction,
accessibility coverage, and hardened evidence/persistence boundaries.

The only compelling product idea to consider borrowing is optional per-app
rhythms, and that belongs after the current accessibility/reliability closeout.
